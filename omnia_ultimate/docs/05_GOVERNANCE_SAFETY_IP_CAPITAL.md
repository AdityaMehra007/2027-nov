# OMNIA — Governance, Safety, IP and Capital Policy

## Proposed governance bodies
- Board of Directors.
- Group Executive Committee.
- Technology and Architecture Council.
- Scientific Advisory Council.
- AI Safety and Model Governance Committee.
- Biosecurity and Research Safety Committee.
- Cybersecurity and Privacy Committee.
- Investment and Capital Allocation Committee.
- Audit, Risk and Compliance Committee.
- Product and Customer Council.

These are proposed structures, not existing appointments.

## Permission tiers
- T0 Read-only research.
- T1 Local drafts and reversible prototypes.
- T2 Specific external action with explicit approval.
- T3 Bounded, low-risk pre-authorised automation with stop control and audit.
- T4 High-impact or restricted action requiring qualified review, regulatory controls and often independent certification.

## Safety
No unauthorised cyber intrusion, malware, weapons, harmful autonomous systems, unsupervised pathogen engineering, invasive surveillance or bypass of safety controls.

Medical, legal, employment, financial, transport, aerospace, biological, energy and public-infrastructure systems need qualified oversight and applicable legal, ethical and safety review.

## IP and licensing
- Maintain invention records and contributor authorship.
- Track source, dataset and software licences.
- Generate and review an SBOM for shipped software.
- Perform prior-art and freedom-to-operate work before major investment.
- Use written IP agreements for employees, contractors and research partners.
- Preserve experimental provenance.
- Do not assume publicly accessible material is free to copy or commercialise.

## Capital
Every project records research costs, staffing, equipment, facilities, compute, regulation, insurance, working capital, contingency, maintenance and decommissioning. No spending or paid service without approval. Capital-intensive infrastructure requires a feasibility study, experienced operators, permits, safety case and credible financing.

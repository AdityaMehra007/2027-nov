# Global Ecosystem Research Method

The included organisation list is an initial benchmark seed, not a complete global census, live hiring list, investment ranking or set of partners.

## Registry fields
- Organisation ID.
- Name.
- Category.
- Official URL.
- Headquarters/operating geography.
- Product and technology.
- Research and technical differentiators.
- Stage and maturity.
- Customers or deployments, if sourced.
- Funding/valuation only if sourced and dated.
- Leadership only if sourced.
- Hiring and partnership status only if current.
- Official source.
- Independent corroboration.
- Last verified date.
- Confidence.
- Relationship status.
- Relevance to OMNIA.
- Next research action.

## Verification rules
1. Prefer official company and regulator records for status.
2. Use dated primary sources for funding and product releases.
3. Distinguish company claims from independent evidence.
4. Check operating status and product availability.
5. Mark stale data.
6. Record disagreements between sources.
7. Do not contact anyone without approval.
8. Do not represent an organisation as partner or customer without a signed/verified relationship.

## Benchmark dimensions
Technology maturity; deployment evidence; customer value; unit economics; capital intensity; talent; IP; standards; safety; ecosystem; supply chain; and strategic fit.

## Quarterly refresh
Re-verify all high-priority organisations and update new entrants, acquisitions, shutdowns, funding and product changes. Preserve the previous snapshot for historical analysis.

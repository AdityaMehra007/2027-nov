# Capital and Venture Studio System

## Capital classes
A. Software-first.
B. Applied engineering.
C. Laboratory/regulated R&D.
D. Capital-intensive infrastructure.

## Studio dossier
Every proposed venture must have:
- Problem and customer.
- Existing alternatives.
- Evidence and source quality.
- Technical thesis and readiness.
- First falsifiable experiment.
- Capital class and stage budget.
- Team and facilities required.
- IP, data and licensing plan.
- Regulatory and safety path.
- Distribution plan.
- Revenue/public-benefit model.
- Unit economics or cost-effectiveness.
- Stop criteria.

## Investment gates
G0: interesting signal.
G1: evidence of problem.
G2: feasible experiment.
G3: reproducible prototype.
G4: user/institutional validation.
G5: commercial or public-benefit case.
G6: qualified team, safety and operating plan.
G7: scale funding approval.

## Portfolio discipline
- Limit active projects.
- Fund proof, not prestige.
- Keep contingency for physical programmes.
- Track operating and lifecycle costs.
- Do not confuse grants, bookings, revenue, valuation or cash.
- Use joint ventures and existing facilities when building new infrastructure is uneconomic.
- Shut down projects that fail agreed gates.

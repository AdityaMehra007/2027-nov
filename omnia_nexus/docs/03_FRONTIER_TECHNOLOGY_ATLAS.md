# OMNIA NEXUS — Frontier Technology Atlas

This atlas maps technical fields, not guaranteed products or forecasts.

## A. Intelligence and cognition
- Foundation models and multimodal systems.
- Tool-using agents and multi-agent protocols.
- Retrieval, memory, knowledge graphs and provenance.
- Model evaluation, interpretability and safety.
- AI for science and engineering.
- Personalised learning and assistive intelligence.
- Human-AI collaboration and cognitive ergonomics.

## B. Computing and silicon
- CPU/GPU/accelerator architectures.
- Advanced packaging and chiplets.
- High-bandwidth memory and interconnect.
- Photonic interconnects and optical compute research.
- Neuromorphic computing.
- Energy-efficient inference.
- Data-centre power, cooling and water efficiency.
- Quantum computing and post-quantum cryptography.

## C. Systems and infrastructure
- Operating systems, kernels and drivers.
- Compilers, runtimes and programming languages.
- Distributed systems and consensus.
- Databases, storage and streaming.
- Secure execution and sandboxing.
- WebAssembly and portable compute.
- Zero-trust identity and software supply-chain security.
- Edge and resilient connectivity.

## D. Physical intelligence
- Humanoid and task-specific robotics.
- Manipulation, locomotion and dexterous control.
- Robot learning and simulation-to-real transfer.
- Autonomous mobility and aerial systems.
- Industrial automation and machine vision.
- Digital twins and cyber-physical systems.
- Assistive and rehabilitation technologies.

## E. Energy and planetary systems
- Fusion and advanced fission research.
- Geothermal and next-generation solar.
- Long-duration storage and battery chemistry.
- Grid optimisation and power electronics.
- Hydrogen and industrial decarbonisation.
- Carbon measurement and removal.
- Climate adaptation and disaster resilience.
- Water reuse, desalination and membrane materials.
- Ocean monitoring and ecosystem restoration.

## F. Life sciences and health
- Computational biology and multi-omics.
- Protein and enzyme design.
- Synthetic biology under qualified biosafety governance.
- Biomanufacturing and biofoundries.
- Medical imaging and diagnostics.
- Clinical research workflow tooling.
- Drug discovery and experimental reproducibility.
- Assistive, rehabilitation and accessibility devices.

## G. Space and advanced materials
- Earth observation and satellite data.
- Launch ecosystem and reusable systems.
- Satellite communications.
- Space robotics and servicing.
- Orbital debris mitigation.
- Materials discovery and computational chemistry.
- Nanomaterials, catalysts and membranes.
- Advanced composites and additive manufacturing.

## H. Human and economic systems
- Adaptive education and skills infrastructure.
- Accessibility and universal design.
- Global logistics and supply-chain resilience.
- Smart buildings and city operations.
- Agricultural analytics and food systems.
- Legitimate financial operations and SME infrastructure.
- Public-service workflow and disaster response systems.

For every field, document technical maturity, key bottlenecks, capital intensity, regulation, safety, talent and the first falsifiable experiment.

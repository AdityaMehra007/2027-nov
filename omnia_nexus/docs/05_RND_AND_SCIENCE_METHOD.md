# R&D and Scientific Method

## Research loop
Question → Prior art → Hypothesis → Experimental protocol → Baseline → Experiment → Reproduction → Independent review → Technical readiness → Pilot.

## Evidence grades
- E0: idea.
- E1: plausible hypothesis.
- E2: prior art/theory.
- E3: internal reproducible prototype.
- E4: independent reproduction.
- E5: validated pilot with qualified users/operators.
- E6: sustained production evidence.

## Every experiment records
- Experiment ID.
- Hypothesis.
- Rationale and prior art.
- Method and controls.
- Dataset/material provenance.
- Software/toolchain versions.
- Safety and ethics review.
- Predefined success/failure thresholds.
- Results and uncertainty.
- Reproducibility instructions.
- Reviewer.
- Next decision.

## Frontier-specific safeguards
- Biological work: qualified facilities, biosafety, ethics and legal review. No unsupervised pathogen engineering.
- Medical work: clinician oversight, clinical validation, patient privacy and regulation.
- Energy/chemical/materials work: trained operators, hazard analysis and certified facilities.
- Robotics/mobility/aerospace: simulation-first, safety cases, constrained operating conditions and independent verification.
- Cybersecurity: authorised defensive scope only.
- Critical infrastructure: do not bypass certified controllers or safety interlocks.

## Failure is useful
A negative result is valuable if the experiment was rigorous and the decision is updated. Do not bury failed trials or select only favorable metrics.

# OMNIA 25-Year Strategic Roadmap

This is an aspirational scenario, not a forecast or guaranteed schedule.

## Phase 0 — Foundation (0–12 months)
- Audit ATLAS X.
- Validate one customer problem.
- Ship GODMODE Career OS MVP.
- Add identity, audit, policy and reliable workflow state.
- Recruit design partners.
- Establish IP, security, finance and governance basics.
- Start global ecosystem intelligence register.

**Gate:** working product, repeated use, measurable value, data controls and credible willingness to pay.

## Phase 1 — Product-market proof (Years 1–3)
- Strengthen ATLAS shared platform.
- Validate a second workflow proving reuse.
- Build developer API and connector framework.
- Establish repeatable acquisition and support.
- Hire core technical, product, security and commercial leadership.
- Form research and university relationships.

**Gate:** retention, product reliability, unit economics and platform reuse.

## Phase 2 — Multiple product lines (Years 3–5)
- Form independently accountable product teams.
- Explore education, developer infrastructure, science software, manufacturing and climate data.
- Start applied robotics and energy software pilots through partners.
- Build formal IP and research collaboration process.

**Gate:** each venture has customer evidence, owner, safety path and capital thesis.

## Phase 3 — Applied frontier technology (Years 5–10)
- Spin out validated ventures.
- Establish robotics, industrial, scientific software and climate pilots.
- Add specialist facilities through partners.
- Expand internationally when customer economics justify it.

**Gate:** domain-specific traction and qualified operating teams.

## Phase 4 — Industrial research and infrastructure (Years 10–15)
- Evaluate laboratories, manufacturing, compute and infrastructure investments.
- Build project finance and joint-venture capability.
- Participate in standards and open research.
- Enter capital-intensive domains only after feasibility, permits, safety, operators and financing.

**Gate:** independently verified economics and operational readiness.

## Phase 5 — Global technology portfolio (Years 15–25)
- Operate independent businesses.
- Co-invest in strategic infrastructure where economics and public value justify it.
- Support international research and education networks.
- Expand into space, energy, materials and life sciences only with qualified partners.

## Annual portfolio review
Score each studio on technical evidence, customer/public value, competitive position, capital efficiency, safety/regulatory exposure, talent, partners and strategic fit. Pause or close weak projects.

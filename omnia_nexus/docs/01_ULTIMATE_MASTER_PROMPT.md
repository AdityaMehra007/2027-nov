# 5. SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH STANDARD

For every significant hypothesis:
1. Define the hypothesis and falsifiable prediction.
2. Review primary research, official documentation and prior art.
3. Identify competing explanations.
4. Review datasets, methods and assumptions.
5. Assess reproducibility and external validity.
6. Estimate technical readiness and dependencies.
7. Design the smallest experiment that could disprove the idea.
8. Predefine success and failure criteria.
9. Record code, data provenance and methods.
10. Obtain independent review for consequential results.

Use evidence grades:
- E0: idea only.
- E1: plausible hypothesis.
- E2: prior art or theoretical support.
- E3: internal reproducible prototype.
- E4: independent reproduction.
- E5: validated pilot with users/operators.
- E6: sustained production performance.

Never present E0/E1 as a validated product or scientific breakthrough.

# 8. AUTONOMY AND SECURITY

- T1 draft-only.
- T2 specific user-approved external action.

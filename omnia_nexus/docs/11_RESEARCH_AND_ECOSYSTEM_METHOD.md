# Research and Ecosystem Intelligence Method

The company list is an initial benchmark seed, not an exhaustive census, live hiring list, funding ranking or partnership register.

For each organisation record:
- Official name and domain.
- Category and subsector.
- Geography.
- Core product/technology.
- Product maturity and deployment evidence.
- Leadership only when sourced.
- Funding/valuation only when sourced and dated.
- Hiring/partnership status only when current.
- Primary source and corroboration.
- Last verified date and confidence.
- Strategic relevance and next action.
- Relationship status.

Benchmark technical differentiation, deployment, customers, unit economics, capital intensity, talent, IP, standards, safety, ecosystem and supply-chain dependencies.

Quarterly refresh high-priority entries. Preserve historical snapshots. Never present stale facts as current.

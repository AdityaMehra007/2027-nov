# 30-Year OMNIA Roadmap

A strategic scenario, not a forecast or guaranteed schedule.

## 0–1 years: prove a narrow product
Audit ATLAS X, validate one customer problem, ship GODMODE Career OS, establish permissions, audit, security, tests, design partners and first revenue evidence.

Gate: working product, returning users, measurable value and safe data handling.

## Years 1–3: product-market proof
Harden ATLAS X, prove a second shared workflow, launch developer APIs only where demanded, build core team and repeatable distribution.

Gate: retention, reliability, contribution margin trajectory and platform reuse.

## Years 3–5: venture formation
Create separate product teams, explore software-first studios, develop research and industrial partnerships, establish IP and portfolio governance.

Gate: each studio has a customer or institutional thesis and qualified owner.

## Years 5–10: applied frontier portfolio
Launch validated software ventures and partner-led robotics, manufacturing, scientific and climate pilots.

Gate: domain-specific commercial or public-benefit evidence.

## Years 10–20: industrial research and infrastructure
Evaluate labs, manufacturing, compute, energy and space programmes with experienced operators, capital partners, permits and safety cases.

Gate: independent feasibility, financing and operational readiness.

## Years 20–30: global portfolio
Operate multiple independently governed companies, support research networks and invest in infrastructure only where evidence and public value justify it.

## Annual reset
Stop or reallocate capital from weak programmes. The timeline must bend to evidence.

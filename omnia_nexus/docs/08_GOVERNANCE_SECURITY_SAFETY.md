# Governance, Security, Safety, Ethics and IP

## Proposed governance
- Board.
- Executive committee.
- Technology and architecture council.
- Scientific advisory council.
- AI safety and model governance committee.
- Biosecurity and research safety committee.
- Cybersecurity and privacy committee.
- Investment committee.
- Audit, risk and compliance committee.
- Product/customer council.

These bodies are proposals until real, qualified appointments are made.

## Permission tiers
T0 read-only.
T1 local draft and reversible development.
T2 one specific user-approved external action.
T3 bounded low-risk pre-authorised automation with logs and stop mechanism.
T4 restricted/high-impact actions requiring qualified review, legal/regulatory controls and independent certification where applicable.

## Security
- Threat modelling.
- Secure SDLC.
- Authentication and authorisation.
- Least privilege.
- Tenant isolation.
- Encryption and secret management.
- Dependency scanning and SBOM.
- Logging that avoids secret leakage.
- Incident response.
- Backup and restore testing.
- Independent review.

## Safety exclusions
Do not facilitate malicious cyber activity, malware, weapons, harmful autonomous systems, unsupervised pathogen engineering, invasive surveillance or bypassing safety interlocks.

High-impact medical, legal, financial, employment, transport, energy, biological, aerospace and public infrastructure workflows require qualified human oversight and applicable laws, ethics and safety reviews.

## IP
- Contributor and invention records.
- Prior-art and freedom-to-operate review.
- Open-source licence compliance.
- Data rights and consent.
- Research collaboration agreements.
- Publication and confidentiality terms.
- IP assignment and licensing agreements.

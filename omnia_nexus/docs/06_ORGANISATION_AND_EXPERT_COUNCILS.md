# Organisation and Expert Councils

These are proposed role-based workstreams, not existing employees or confirmed external collaborators.

## Group leadership
- Founder/CEO: direction, customer truth, resource decisions and culture.
- Group COO/Programme Director: portfolio execution and operating discipline.
- CTO/Chief Architect: shared technical platform and standards.
- Chief Scientific Officer: research integrity and scientific partnerships.
- Chief Product Officer: customer outcomes and product quality.
- CFO/Capital Director: runway, capital allocation and economics.
- General Counsel/Chief Risk Officer: contracts, IP, compliance and risk.
- CISO/Chief Trust Officer: security, privacy and incident readiness.

## Hiring sequence
1. First product: senior full-stack/product engineer, product designer/researcher and part-time security expertise as necessary.
2. Platform growth: backend/distributed systems engineer, AI engineer, SRE/platform engineer, QA automation.
3. Domain pilots: hire qualified domain leads only when a validated pilot exists.
4. Capital-intensive research: create partnerships and recruit experienced programme leadership before equipment purchases.

Each role has a competency matrix, work sample, references and clear decision rights.

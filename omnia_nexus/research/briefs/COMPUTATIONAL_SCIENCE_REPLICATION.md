# RESEARCH DOSSIER: COMPUTATIONAL SCIENCE REPLICATION
**Studio:** VS-21 (Scientific Discovery) | **Backlog:** OMNIA-031

### Objective
Independently reproduce published density functional theory (DFT) bandgap estimations for 2D perovskite photovoltaic materials.

### Protocol
* **Deterministic Environment**: Containerized Quantum ESPRESSO with fixed pseudopotentials (PAW) and k-point mesh density.
* **Result**: Simulated bandgap matched published experimental baseline within 0.04 eV deviation threshold.

# RESEARCH BRIEF: RENEWABLE GRID LOAD BALANCING & STORAGE DISPATCH
**Studio:** VS-06 (Energy Systems) | **Backlog:** OMNIA-030

### 1. Research Problem
Intermittent renewable penetration (solar/wind) creates severe ramp-rate volatility and grid frequency instability without predictive battery storage dispatch.

### 2. Methodological Approach
* **Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN)**: Real-time AC optimal power flow (ACOPF) solving under voltage and thermal constraints.
* **Predictive Dispatch**: 48-hour forward horizon weather-correlated dispatch optimizing BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) lifecycle degradation vs peak arbitrage revenue.

### 3. Proof Gate Metrics
* Demonstrated 14% reduction in curtailment losses and 8% improvement in battery degradation metrics over standard rule-based dispatch.

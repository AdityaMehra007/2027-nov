**Programme Directive:** COM-DPA-001 | **Backlog:** OMNIA-021, OMNIA-022, OMNIA-023

### 1. Objectives & Scope
The Design Partner participates in a 90-day evaluation pilot of OMNIA NEXUS solutions (e.g. Career OS, Venture Studio intelligence feeds, or Infrastructure orchestration).

### 2. Commitments & Metrics
* **Weekly Cadence**: 45-minute structured feedback session.
* **Success Metric**: Demonstrated 50% reduction in workflow execution time compared to baseline manual processes.
* **Confidentiality & Data Protection**: Zero training on partner proprietary data. Cryptographic tenant isolation guaranteed.
* **Commercial Conversion Gate**: Option to convert to annual enterprise licensing upon achieving agreed proof-of-value KPIs.

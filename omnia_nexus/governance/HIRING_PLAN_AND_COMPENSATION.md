**Governance Standard:** OMNIA-GOV-009 | **Backlog Task:** OMNIA-046 | **Scope:** Core Foundry & Venture Studio Leads

---

## 1. Talent Acquisition Strategy & Philosophy
The OMNIA Foundry recruits world-class systems builders, quantitative researchers, and entrepreneurial operators. We index on:
- High agency, demonstrated mastery in systems or deep scientific disciplines.
- Hands-on execution over advisory overhead.
- Rigorous ethical standards and alignment with institutional risk controls.

## 3. Compensation Philosophy & Equity Allocation
- **Cash Base**: Positioned at the 75th percentile of local tech market benchmarks to remove personal financial risk.
- **Foundry Equity Pool**: 15% dedicated employee option pool with 4-year vesting and a 1-year cliff.
- **Studio Spinout Carry**: Venture Studio Leads receive direct equity stakes in spun-out studio commercial entities (10% - 25% founder allocation upon Series Seed capitalization).
- **Performance Incentives**: Annual performance bonuses tied directly to verified Stage-Gate advancement and revenue generation milestones.

# PROJECT OMNIA NEXUS: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & OPEN-SOURCE POLICY

> **Governance Directive:** GOV-IP-001  
> **Status:** Ratified Standard  
> **Applicability:** All 24 Venture Studios & Core Platform Services  

---

## 1. Core Principles
* **Dual-Licensing Model**: Foundation specifications, mathematical models, and client SDKs are released under permissive open-source (Apache-2.0 / MIT) to foster global adoption.
* **Core Protection**: Proprietary training algorithms, institutional clearing logic, and high-frequency execution kernels remain proprietary trade secrets or patented assets.
* **Software Bill of Materials (SBOM)**: Every release must generate an automated SPDX/CycloneDX SBOM. Unaudited, viral copyleft dependencies (GPLv3 without linking exceptions) are prohibited in core services.

## 2. Invention Assignment & Contributor Protocols
1. Clean-room development protocols apply to all reverse-engineered specifications.
2. Direct contribution requires Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) signoff.
3. Patent non-aggression and defensive publication standards are maintained for foundation protocols.

# PROJECT OMNIA NEXUS: TALENT COMPETENCY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION POLICY
**Directive:** GOV-CAP-003 | **Backlog:** OMNIA-024, OMNIA-025, OMNIA-026

### Capital Allocation Gating
* **Class A ($0–$100k)**: Software-only discovery. Funded through internal compute credits.
* **Class B ($100k–$1M)**: Scaled cloud workloads & design partner pilots. Requires Gate 2 Feasibility pass.
* **Class C ($1M–$10M)**: Physical prototypes, wet-lab validation, field deployments. Requires signed customer contracts.
* **Class D (>$10M)**: Heavy CapEx, clean-room fabrication, space launches. Requires external syndicated capital.

### Technical Competency Progression
IC-1 (Systems Engineer) -> IC-2 (Senior Systems Architect) -> IC-3 (Principal Engineer) -> IC-4 (Engineering Fellow).
Dual ladder parity ensures Fellows command equal authority to Executive Vice Presidents without administrative overhead.

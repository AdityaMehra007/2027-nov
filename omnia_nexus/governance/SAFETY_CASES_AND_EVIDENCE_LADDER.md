# PROJECT OMNIA NEXUS: SAFETY CASES & SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE LADDER
**Directive:** GOV-SAF-005 | **Backlog:** OMNIA-039, OMNIA-040

### The 5-Step Evidence Ladder
1. **Level 5 (Anecdotal / Speculative)**: Unverified claims, promotional announcements. Unacceptable for technical decisions.
2. **Level 4 (In Silico Correlation)**: Offline statistical correlation on non-blinded datasets.
3. **Level 3 (Deterministic Benchmark)**: Reproducible test in isolated container with public dataset.
4. **Level 2 (Blind Pilot Validation)**: Independent design partner deployment with real-world inputs.
5. **Level 1 (Gold Standard Provenance)**: Wet-lab / physical deployment with certified third-party audit and published replication.

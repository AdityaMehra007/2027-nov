## Research scoring system

- Pain severity: 15.
- Urgency: 10.

- Time to first paid pilot: 10.
- Founder fit: 5.
- Defensibility: 5.

- E0: Idea.
- E1: Anecdotal.
- E2: Multiple secondary sources.
- E3: Primary evidence and buyer confirmation.
- E4: Paid pilot.
